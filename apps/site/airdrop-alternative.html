AirDrop for Windows, Android and everything else

Windows, Android और सबके लिए AirDrop

AirDrop is great, until the other person has a Windows laptop or an Android phone. Narad is the cross‑platform AirDrop alternative that runs in the browser: devices on the same Wi‑Fi find each other instantly, files go straight across your router, and anyone further away gets a 6‑digit code. No app, no account, no size limit.AirDrop अच्छा है, जब तक सामने वाले के पास Windows लैपटॉप या Android फ़ोन न हो। Narad ब्राउज़र में चलने वाला cross‑platform AirDrop विकल्प है: एक ही Wi‑Fi पर डिवाइस तुरंत एक‑दूसरे को देखते हैं, फाइलें सीधे राउटर से जाती हैं, और दूर वालों को 6 अंकों का कोड। न app, न अकाउंट, न size limit।

FAQ

Is there AirDrop for Windows?

Not from Apple. Narad gives Windows the same experience in the browser: open ${APP_HOST} on the PC and on the iPhone (same Wi‑Fi), they see each other, drag a file across. No install.

How do I AirDrop from iPhone to Android?

Open Narad on both. On the same Wi‑Fi they appear in each other’s list automatically; otherwise share a 6‑digit code. Photos keep full quality: nothing is recompressed.

Does it work without internet?

On the same Wi‑Fi the bytes go through your router, not the internet. A signaling server is still needed once to introduce the devices; the self‑host edition runs that inside your office for fully offline networks.

Is it as fast as AirDrop?

On the same Wi‑Fi you get your router’s speed, typically 20–80 MB/s. AirDrop uses a direct Wi‑Fi link so it can be faster for huge files between two Apple devices; across platforms it simply does not exist.

Does Narad work on Linux and Chromebook?

Yes. Anything with a modern browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari, Brave.

Got a code?कोड मिला?

Type the 6‑digit code the sender gave you. The app opens and the transfer starts, device to device.भेजने वाले का 6 अंकों का कोड डालें। App खुलेगा और transfer डिवाइस‑से‑डिवाइस शुरू होगा।
