Share files with anyone, with just a code

किसी को भी फाइल भेजो, बस एक कोड से

No email attachments, no expiring links, no “please create an account to download”. Narad gives you a 6‑digit code. How to share a file with a codeकोड से फाइल कैसे भेजें

  1. Open app.narad.sh in any browser (phone or computer).किसी भी ब्राउज़र में app.narad.sh खोलें (फ़ोन या कंप्यूटर)।
  2. Tap Share with code. Add files, a folder, or type / paste text. Tap Get code.कोड से भेजें दबाएँ। फाइल, फ़ोल्डर जोड़ें या टेक्स्ट लिखें / paste करें। कोड बनाएँ दबाएँ।
  3. Tell the other person the 6 digits, or send them the link / QR shown under the code.सामने वाले को 6 अंक बताएँ, या कोड के नीचे दिखा लिंक / QR भेजें।
  4. They open ${HOST}/receive, type it, tap Accept. Done.वे ${HOST}/receive खोलें, कोड डालें, Accept दबाएँ। बस।

The code stays live for 15 minutes or until you tap Stop sharing. Every person who types it gets the same items over their own encrypted connection, and you see “Delivered to …” for each one.कोड 15 मिनट या आपके भेजना बंद करें दबाने तक चलता है। जो भी डाले, उसे वही चीज़ें अपने encrypted कनेक्शन पर मिलती हैं, और आपको हर एक के लिए “पहुँच गया: …” दिखता है।

Turn a one‑time share into a contactएक बार के share को contact बनाएँ

After the transfer, either side can tap Add as contact. Under the hood both devices already ran a password‑authenticated key exchange (SPAKE2) on the code, so the contact is bound to the real devices, not to the code. From then on you see each other online on any network and share without codes, with optional auto‑accept and clipboard sync.Transfer के बाद कोई भी तरफ़ संपर्क में जोड़ें दबा सकती है। अंदर‑अंदर दोनों डिवाइस कोड पर SPAKE2 चला चुके होते हैं, इसलिए contact असली डिवाइस से बँधता है, कोड से नहीं। अब से आप किसी भी नेटवर्क पर एक‑दूसरे को online देखते हैं और बिना कोड share करते हैं, optional auto‑accept और clipboard sync के साथ।

FAQ

Can I share files with someone who doesn’t have an account?

Yes, that is the whole point. Narad has no accounts. You get a 6‑digit code; they type it at ${HOST}/receive in any browser. Neither side installs anything.

Can several people use the same code?

Yes. While the code is open (up to 15 minutes) everyone who types it gets the same files, each over their own encrypted connection. Handy for a meeting room or a classroom.

Is a 6‑digit code safe?

The code only names a rendezvous room that exists for minutes; the content is encrypted end to end between the devices and the server rate‑limits joins. Both sides also see four emojis that must match. For long‑term sharing, add the person as a contact, which upgrades the session with SPAKE2 so the code can never be replayed.

What can I share with a code?

Files of any size, whole folders, text, links, screenshots, or whatever is on your clipboard.

Does the receiver need Wi‑Fi with me?

No. Codes work across the internet. On the same Wi‑Fi you don’t even need a code: devices find each other automatically.

Got a code?कोड मिला?

Type the 6‑digit code the sender gave you. The app opens and the transfer starts, device to device.भेजने वाले का 6 अंकों का कोड डालें। App खुलेगा और transfer डिवाइस‑से‑डिवाइस शुरू होगा।
